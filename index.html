MeshDoctor v1.6.37

The mesh points define the correction boundary. MeshDoctor can straighten photographed paperwork or keep a full photo framed for restoration.

AI Assisted uses secure AI image restoration when available. If it is unavailable, MeshDoctor automatically uses its on-device restoration fallback.

Create a PDF lets you combine images with selected pages from existing PDFs, omit pages you do not want, reorder everything, edit pages through MeshDoctor, and save a new PDF.

v1.6.37: adds an exact last-HQ-capture preview card, tightens the ghost to an inward-safe strip from the real HQ crop, sizes the ghost by the actual captured strip aspect so it no longer appears to show extra unseen label area, re-seeds the first live panorama from the first accepted HQ frame, and trims loop-tail duplicates before final stitching.

v1.6.36: fixes full-rotation detection by fingerprinting the first accepted HQ capture and using shift-tolerant correlation to stop on the first genuine return to the starting label area instead of continuing into a second lap or false-triggering on a different text/front panel.

v1.6.35: hardens live Label Maker tracking around dense/repeating text, rejects implausible tracking jumps, freezes the ghost to the last accepted HQ capture only, pauses live tracking during the HQ shutter, and builds final panorama pixels/placement primarily from the accepted HQ keyframes instead of trusting a corrupted live map.
